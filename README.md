**Evaluating Machine Unlearning Techniques for Selective Knowledge Removal**

Supervisor: Antu Chowdhury

## Contents
- `paper/` — Final manuscript (PDF)
- `code/` — Kaggle notebooks (ELM and PISCES experiments)

## Summary
This study evaluates two concept-erasure methods, ELM (fine-tuning-based) 
and PISCES (activation-intervention-based), on Gemma-2-2B-it under 
single-concept and multi-concept (joint) erasure settings, and introduces 
the Concept Interference Score (CIS) to quantify degradation caused by 
joint erasure.
